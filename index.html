What We Offer

Everything Your Society
Needs in One App

From visitor logging to panic alerts — Society Secure covers every corner of your gated community.

01
🔐

Visitor Management

Security guards can log visitor entries with name, phone, flat number, and purpose. Full audit trail maintained digitally — no more paper registers.

02
📱

QR Pass System

Residents pre-approve guests with a unique QR code. Guards scan or verify the pass code at the gate — instant, paperless, fraud-proof entry.

03
🚨

Panic / Emergency Alert

One tap sends an SOS to all security guards with your location. Emergency contacts for Police (100), Fire (101) and society office always at hand.

04
🔧

Complaints & Issues

Residents raise maintenance complaints — water leakage, electrical faults, etc. — tagged with priority level (Low / Medium / High). Track status in real time.

05
💳

Maintenance Payments

Pay society bills digitally. Track total paid, pending, and overall billing history — no more knocking on the committee door.

06
🏊

Amenity Booking

Book clubhouse, gym, swimming pool, and more from your phone. View your bookings, manage slots — zero friction, 24/7 available.
