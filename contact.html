FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I onboard my society? +

Simply fill out the contact form above or reach us by phone. Our team will schedule a free demo and help you set up within 48 hours. No technical expertise needed.

Is Society Secure free to use? +

We offer a free trial for the first 30 days with full features. After that, affordable monthly plans are available based on society size. Contact us for pricing details.

Do security guards need training? +

No. The Security Guard app is designed to be extremely simple — even for users with basic smartphone skills. Most guards are comfortable within the first day of use.

Is resident data secure? +

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption, secure servers, and follow data privacy best practices. Your society's data is never shared with third parties.
